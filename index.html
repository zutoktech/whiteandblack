(09) FAQ

Questions we often get

Still curious? Call or WhatsApp +91 98423 55203.

What does White & Black do?

White & Black is a creative agency and print partner in Trichy, Tamil Nadu. Since 2006 we have designed logos and brand identities, packaging, business cards, brochures, hoardings and social media creatives, and we handle offset and digital printing.

Where is White & Black located?

Our studio is at D46A, ABC Lotus Arcade, 6th Cross, Thillai Nagar East Extension, Trichy 620018, Tamil Nadu. Call +91 98423 55203 or email mail@whiteandblack.in.

Do you only design, or do you print as well?

Both. We design the artwork and manage the printing, so brochures, cards, packaging and outdoor material come out exactly as designed.

Which printed products can you make?

Books and diaries, brochures, business cards, catalogues, danglers, directories, files, flyers, invitations, magazines, menus, packaging, posters, souvenirs and wrappers, plus hoardings and standees.

Do you work with businesses outside Trichy?

Yes. We work with corporate and commercial brands across Tamil Nadu and share designs and proofs online.

How does your design process work?

Four steps: Learn about your brand and needs, Plan the direction after careful analysis, Design with deliberate execution, and Execute after an expert review.

How long has White & Black been in business?

We have been designing and printing for brands since 2006, with over 20 years of design experience in the team.

How do I get a quote?

Call or WhatsApp +91 98423 55203, email mail@whiteandblack.in, or send your requirement through the quote form on this website.
