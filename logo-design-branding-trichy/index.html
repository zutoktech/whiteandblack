FAQ

Logo Design questions

Still curious? Call or WhatsApp +91 98423 55203.

How does your logo design process work?

We follow four steps: Learn about your business and audience, Plan the direction, Design and refine concepts, then Execute with an expert review before sharing the final logo.

What do I receive at the end of a logo project?

You receive final logo files for print and digital use, and we can extend the logo into a full identity with colours, fonts, stationery and signage.

Can you refresh an old logo instead of starting over?

Yes. If your logo already has recognition, we can modernise it while keeping the elements your customers know.

Do you design logos for businesses outside Trichy?

Yes. We work with businesses across Tamil Nadu and share concepts online for review.
