FAQ

Packaging questions

Still curious? Call or WhatsApp +91 98423 55203.

Do you design and print packaging in Trichy?

Yes. White & Black is a creative agency and print partner in Thillai Nagar, Trichy. We design the packaging artwork and manage the printing, so you work with one team from the first idea to the finished boxes.

Can you redesign our existing packaging?

Yes. We study your current pack, the products next to it on the shelf and your customer, then refresh the design so it stays recognisable but stands out more.

What details do you need to quote for packaging?

Share the product, the box or label size, the quantity, the number of variants and any finish you have in mind. A photo of your current pack helps us quote faster.

Do you work with brands outside Trichy?

Yes. We work with brands across Tamil Nadu and share designs and proofs online, so your location is never a barrier.
